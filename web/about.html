About & sources

Explore market-value estimates and illustrative transfer-fee scenarios across the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A and Ligue 1.

What the numbers mean

The transfer-fee view applies a 33.3% premium to a model estimate or a clearly labelled dated reference. The premium comes from a selected 25-player reference sample. It has not been validated as a transfer-fee model and is not a confirmed fee, offer or guarantee. Elite players and unusual circumstances can differ substantially from these estimates.

Current estimates combine a dated preseason valuation with age, position, league, minutes, goals and cards. Model estimates require at least 180 verified league minutes and an unambiguous identity match. Players without sufficient supporting data show a dated reference or no price. Fantasy game prices and fantasy assists are not used.

Freshness and currency

While the server is awake, it checks for updates and refreshes data older than six hours. A free host can sleep when idle; checks resume on waking. Recent cached data remains available during source failures, but snapshots older than 48 hours are hidden. Look at each league’s fetch date rather than assuming real-time coverage.

Amounts are in pounds using the model’s fixed conversion rate of £0.85950 per euro. Updates change match data, not the frozen model, historical valuations, conversion rate or fee premium. The current experimental model supports September–April within the season; rebuilding the valuation inputs and checking model validity is necessary for a later season.

Sources and attribution

  • Official Fantasy Premier League: Premier League statistics. Appearance counts and comparable assists are unavailable in this integration.
  • ESPN: public squad and match-statistics feeds for the other four leagues. These endpoints are not a guaranteed supported API.
  • Transfermarkt via dcaribou/transfermarkt-datasets: historical statistics, identities and dated valuations. The community publisher declares its dataset CC0. This is not an official Transfermarkt API.
  • The alternative collector uses football-data.org: Football data provided by the Football-Data.org API. The current live page uses the sources above.

Touchline is an independent project and is not endorsed by these providers. No player photos or club crests are included. Public access to a source does not itself establish permission for every reuse; source attribution is retained.
