Privacy

Touchline lets you browse player estimates without creating an account.

Your saved shortlist

Player IDs and names that you save are stored in this browser’s local storage. Prices are fetched again when you open the app. Removing saved players or clearing this site’s browser data removes that stored shortlist. It does not sync between devices.

Requests and logs

Search terms, filters and selected player IDs are sent to the Touchline server to return results. The application has no advertising trackers or analytics scripts and sets no application cookies. Its hosting provider may process connection information such as IP addresses, requested URLs and timestamps for operation and security. Hosting logs are subject to that provider’s policies.

External sources

Football data is collected by the server from the sources listed on the About & sources page. Opening an external source link takes you to a site with its own privacy policy. Browser shortlists are not sent to those football providers.
